I have updated my Common Lisp book over thirty times with new material since I published it a decade ago. My message to you is this: if you are reading this book for free online, please revisit the book link periodically for new material. If you bought this book and asked for notifications of new versions, then you will receive an email whenever I produce a new edition with a free download link.
